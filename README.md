# Happy 23rd Birthday Dr. Chandana! 🌸✨

A romantic, modern, aesthetic single-section 23rd birthday web celebration created with endless love for Dr. Chandana.

Hosted live on GitHub Pages: **`https://moulyyy.github.io/Birthday/`**

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

### 🎂 Single-Section Birthday Ceremony (`index.html`)
- **Modern Minimal Luxury Aesthetic**: Glassmorphism, smooth gradients, subtle ambient particles, and responsive typography designed to fit seamlessly on mobile and desktop without clutter.
- **3-Storey Grand Birthday Cake**:
  - Piped cursive calligraphy icing on **all 3 tiers** (Top, Middle, and Bottom):
    - *Top Tier*: `Happy Birthday Dr Chandana`
    - *Middle Tier*: `Happy Birthday Dr Chandana`
    - *Bottom Tier*: `Happy Birthday Dr Chandana 💕`
  - Real blowable number "23" candle + taper candles with realistic smoke puffs and candle flame flickers.
- **Dynamic Singing Popcat (`popcat.gif`)**:
  - Starts hidden for a sleek, clean appearance.
  - Dynamically appears with a royal party hat, speech bubble, and floating musical notes when candle blowing begins.
  - Synchronized with `cat_birthday.mp3` singing audio.
  - Celebrates during cake slicing, then smoothly disappears after the cake cutting is done, while the audio fades out gracefully.
- **Interactive Cake Slicing**:
  - Realistic draggable interactive knife with dashed slice guidelines.
  - "Slice The Cake!" action button as an accessible alternative.
  - Slices a strawberry cake wedge onto a gilded porcelain serving dish with confetti and fireworks showers.
- **Live Relationship Love Counter**:
  - Live timer tracking Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds together since 6th June 2025.
  - Dynamic milestone indicator ("1 Year, 3 Months & X Days of Pure Magic Together").
- **Romantic Celebration Banner & Replay**:
  - Heartfelt 23rd birthday message dedicated to Dr. Chandana.
  - "Relive The Cake Cut" button allows the celebration to be replayed anytime.

---

## 🎵 Audio Experience
- **Cat Birthday Song**: `cat_birthday.mp3` plays during candle blowing with the singing Popcat, and fades out gently after cake cutting.
- **Web Audio Engine**: Provides realistic SFX for candle blowing, knife slicing, chimes, fireworks bursts, and ambient melody.

---

## 📱 Cross-Device Responsiveness
- **Desktop & Laptops**: Centered single-section layout with ambient lighting and particle effects.
- **Mobile & Tablets**: Optimized touch drag-and-drop for knife slicing, tap-to-blow candles, and responsive fluid text.
