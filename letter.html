A Little Note for You ❤️

To my favourite person,

Somehow, in the middle of all the chaos of life, I found you. And since then, ordinary days have become a little more special, calls have become something I look forward to, and my life has had a lot more laughs, butterflies, and love.

You have this funny way of being cute, stubborn, clumsy, and completely impossible to stay angry at. You annoy me, make me laugh, challenge me, and somehow still manage to make everything feel better just by being there.

These 471 days with you have given me memories I know I'll carry for a very long time. From our dates and late-night conversations to the smallest, most random moments that only we understand — I wouldn't trade any of them.

And this is only the beginning. I hope we get to create hundreds more memories, annoy each other for decades, grow old together, and one day look back at this little website and laugh at how young and crazy we were.

Until then, keep being my cute, clumsy potato. 🥔

Forever and always yours,

With All My Heart 💕
